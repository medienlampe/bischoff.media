Hi, I'm Sven and I've been working with technology since my youth – first as a hobby and later as a profession too.

It started with a modding website based on vBulletin, which I ran together with friends. That's how I taught myself PHP, then also created my own themes based on existing ones and discovered my joy in graphic design. Later we moved to our own root server. The first machine ran Windows Server, which showed me what I definitely don't like. In 2007 we switched to a new system, this time with Debian. For that I picked up the necessary skills, and to this day I really love working in terminals.

My fascination for web development hasn't left me since: I enjoy designing and creating content, designs and technologies on the web – starting with PHP, JavaScript and Symfony, through the web server and its Linux operating system, all the way down to the hardware, including automation, backups, virtualisation, containers and, for some time now, AI. Here I'm mainly interested in local inference.

Professionally, I've been working at Invia Travel Germany GmbH since 2016 – from Junior Software Developer through Technical Product Owner and Lead Software Engineer to my current role. As Senior Engineering Manager I'm currently responsible for two departments:

  • "Cluster Book" covers the booking steps, the post-sales services and the login.
  • "Cluster App" covers the complete user journey in our iOS and Android apps.

Both belong to ab-in-den-urlaub.de and several other travel portals. I'm supported by four great people: three Engineering Managers and an IT Architect. For my role I've trained in, among other things, leading teams, conflict management and as a Mental Health First Aider. Whether in a one-on-one conversation or in front of a larger audience – support always comes first for me: I'm happiest when people trust me and I see others grow beyond themselves.

Besides that I'm interested in gaming, photography, music, science and do-it-yourself, like to research things that intrigue me and hold a B.Sc. in Medieninformatik from the Harz University of Applied Sciences, where I wrote my bachelor thesis on "The Fragile Presence of the Author in Photography".
